# Business Support

For billing and business support (account setup, invoices, organization questions), email [billing-support@baymax.dev](mailto:billing-support@baymax.dev). Business support is prioritibaymax relative to other support channels.

For general questions, email [hi@baymax.dev](mailto:hi@baymax.dev).

## Open-source issues

Questions and bugs about the Baymax editor itself (features, extensions, language support, crashes) go through the main Baymax project on [GitHub](https://github.com/simtropolis/baymax/issues).
