# Port matrix: Zed v1.11.3

## Decision rules

Each independently reviewable change has exactly one decision. ZUP-091 is split because its license and workflow-safety hunks are unrelated. Decisions use actual patch/tree evidence, patch identity, subsystem review, and the authoritative local filesystem.

- `port unchanged`: carry upstream behavior and tests without changing semantics while preserving unrelated local edits.
- `port with Sim-specific adaptation`: translate or integrate at an intentional Sim architecture or product seam.
- `already present or equivalent`: verified local symbols or sibling-branch patch identity already supply endpoint behavior.
- `prerequisite-only`: retain compatibility or final metadata without claiming separate user behavior.
- `not applicable to Sim`: no Sim counterpart exists, or the change is fully reverted/intermediate release state.
- `deferred with an explicit blocker`: implementation and validation require authority or evidence that is unavailable and expressly out of scope.
- No entry is uncertain, conflicting, or silently omitted.

The compact phrase “pre-change behavior remained” is row-specific shorthand:
the exact capability or fix named in that row's `After` field was absent and
the listed upstream symbols still had their v1.10.2 behavior. `Endpoint
relation` is the pre-port Sim snapshot: `base` means byte-identical to the
v1.10.2 blob, `diverged` means a Sim-modified counterpart was inspected, and
`absent` means the upstream path required a Sim shell mapping or an explicit
non-application decision. Detailed before/after symbols and test hunks remain
in the corresponding inventory entry, rather than being hidden by the commit
subject.
